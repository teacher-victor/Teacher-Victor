Tonight's plan

It is Saturday night and you have a reservation at Luigi's Kitchen. You called on Thursday to book a table for two, at seven o'clock. Your friend Sam is vegetarian, and you do not like spicy food, so read the menu carefully.

A friend recommended the mushroom pasta, and the specials are on the board near the door. The grilled chicken comes with one side. People say the portions are huge, so maybe you will not have room for dessert.

If there is a problem, for example your food is cold or overcooked, tell the server politely. Start with "Excuse me" or "I would like to..." and stay calm.

At the end, ask for the check and read it carefully. Is the tip included? Is every line correct? If there is food on the table, ask for the leftovers to go. By the way, a water refill is free here.

Rehearsal

The Five Courses of a Restaurant Conversation

A restaurant visit always has the same five moments. If you know them, you always know what comes next and what to say. Think of them as five small courses: Arrive, Ask, Order, Fix, Pay.
CourseWhat you doYour key sentence
1 · ArriveSay hello, give your name and the number of people.Hi, we have a reservation for two, under ...
2 · AskAsk two questions about the menu before you choose.What are the specials? Is the ... spicy?
3 · OrderOrder food and drinks with "I'd like" or "I'll have".I'd like the ..., please. And for my friend, ...
4 · FixSay the problem softly, then say what you want.Excuse me, I'm afraid ... Could you ...?
5 · PayAsk for the check, read it, ask about the tip.Could we have the check, please?

Model conversation

Read it aloud with a partner. The numbers show the five courses.

ServerGood evening, and welcome to Luigi's Kitchen!

You · 1Hi, we have a reservation for two, under Silva.

ServerSilva... yes, here it is. Table six. Here are your menus.

You · 2Thank you. What are the specials tonight?

ServerTonight we have a seafood risotto, for nineteen dollars.

You · 2I see. And is the sausage pizza very spicy?

ServerYes, it is quite spicy. The mushroom pasta is not spicy at all.

You · 3Great. I'd like the mushroom pasta, please. And my friend will have the vegetable lasagna.

ServerGood choice. Anything to drink?

You · 3One lemonade and one water, please.

ServerHere you are. Enjoy your meal!

You · 4Excuse me, I'm afraid my pasta is cold. Could you bring me a hot one, please?

ServerOh, I'm so sorry about that. I'll bring you a new one right away.

You · 5Thank you. Everything was delicious. Could we have the check, please?

ServerOf course. Here it is.

You · 5Sorry, I think there is a mistake. We ordered one lemonade, not two.

ServerYou are right. I'll fix that for you now.
