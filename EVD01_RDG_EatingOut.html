Review 1

Luigi's Kitchen

★★★★★$$
ItalianOpen until 11 PMWindow tables

We went to Luigi's on a Friday night without a reservation, and we waited twenty minutes for a table. It was worth the wait! The menu is small, but there are many good choices. I ordered the mushroom pasta and my husband had some grilled chicken with vegetables.

The portions are huge, so we didn't have any room for dessert. There isn't much space between the tables and the restaurant gets noisy after eight o'clock, but the staff are friendly and fast. Our waiter recommended the pasta, and he was right.

The prices are reasonable: we paid about $18 per person. My tip: book a table if you would like to sit by the window.

Idioms & Set Phrases 4

worth the wait
set phrase

So good that waiting for it is not a problem.

The line was long, but the pizza was worth the wait.

room for dessert
idiom

Space in your stomach to eat something sweet after a meal.

I ate too much pasta. I have no room for dessert.

book a table
set phrase

To ask a restaurant to keep a table for you.

Let's book a table for Friday night.

not worth the money
set phrase

Too expensive for how good it is.

Useful Expressions 4

I would like to
polite expression

A polite way to say "I want to".

I would like to order the fish, please.

By the way
discourse marker

Use it to add a new, extra piece of information.

By the way, the restaurant is closed on Mondays.

To be fair
discourse marker

Use it before a good point, after you said something negative.

The room was small. To be fair, it was very clean.

In the end
discourse marker

Use it to say what happened finally, after thinking or waiting.

We looked at three hotels. In the end, we chose the cheapest one.

Grammar

Some, any, much, many and "would like"

Why it matters: when you talk about food and restaurants, you talk about things you can count (tables, dishes) and things you cannot count (space, bread, money). You also need a polite way to say what you want. These two tools are in almost every restaurant conversation.

1. Can you count it?

CountableUncountable
What is it?Separate things. You can say one, two, three.A mass or an idea. You cannot say one, two, three.
Examplesa table, two dishes, three desserts, six peoplespace, bread, rice, parking, time, money
Plural?Yes: table, tablesNo: breads, moneys
QuestionHow many tables are there?How much time did we wait?

2. The forms

some = positive  ·  any = negative and questions
many + countable  ·  much + uncountable
UseFormExample from this lesson
Positive sentencesome + countable plural or uncountableIt has some vegan desserts. We ordered some fish.
Negative sentencenot ... any + nounThey don't serve any meat. There isn't any parking.
A big numbermany + countable pluralThere are many good choices.
A small numbernot many + countable pluralThere aren't many tables.
A small amountnot much + uncountableThere isn't much space. We don't have much money.
Polite wish (thing)would like + nounI would like a table for six, please.
Polite wish (action)would like to + verbI would like to try that mushroom pasta.
Short formI'd like = I would likeI'd like some bread, please.
Why reviews use this grammar: a review answers simple questions. How many tables? How much money? Is there any parking? Are there some vegetarian dishes? And "would like" is softer than "want", so it sounds friendly when you give advice or order food.
