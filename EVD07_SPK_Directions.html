Role A · Help Point volunteer

You know the city. Give clear, short instructions with a landmark and a time.

Role B · Visitor

It is your first day. Ask politely, listen, and repeat the directions to check.

Today you are a volunteer at the Kingsbridge Tourist Help Point, in front of the train station. Visitors come with questions all day. Your job is to give clear directions: tell them to go straight on, cross the road, or walk along the river, and give them a landmark, like the horse statue in Market square.

Some visitors walk. Tell them how long it takes on foot and where the entrance is. Others take the metro: tell them which platform, where to get off, and if a day ticket is better. If they look lost, smile and say "Don't worry."

Then change roles. Now you are the visitor. Start with "Excuse me", ask "How long does it take?", and repeat the directions to check: "Left at the traffic lights, then it's opposite the library... Got it!" If the place is behind something, ask for a landmark. A good answer often ends with: "You can't miss it."

Rehearsal

Start, Steps, Check

Good directions have three parts. Start: say where you are and how far it is. Steps: two or three short instructions, each with a landmark. Check: the visitor repeats, and you say "Yes, that's right".
PartVolunteer (Role A)Visitor (Role B)
1 · StartWe are here. It's about five minutes on foot.Excuse me, how do I get to ...?
2 · StepsGo straight on ... Turn left at ... It's opposite ...Listen. Ask: How long does it take?
3 · CheckYes, that's right. / No, left, not right.So, left at the lights, then ...? Got it, thanks!

Model role-play: Mission 1

Visitor · StartExcuse me. How do I get to Market Square, please?

Volunteer · StartMarket Square? It's not far. We are here, at the station. It's about five minutes on foot.

Volunteer · StepsGo out of the station and turn right. Walk along King Street to the traffic lights. Then turn left into Bridge Road and walk up to the end.

Visitor · StepsHow do I know it's Market Square?

Volunteer · StepsThere is a big horse statue in the middle. You can't miss it.

Visitor · CheckSo, right out of the station, along King Street, left at the lights, and up Bridge Road to the end?

Volunteer · CheckYes, that's right!

Visitor · CheckGot it. Thanks a lot!

VolunteerYou're welcome. Enjoy the city!
